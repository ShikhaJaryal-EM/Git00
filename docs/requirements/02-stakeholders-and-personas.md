# Stakeholders & Personas

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Approved

## Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Interest |
|-------------|----------|
| **Office / HR Administrator** | Maintain an accurate, up-to-date employee list |

## Persona: Office Administrator

**Role:** Office or HR administrator

### Goals

- Add new employees
- Update employee names or email addresses
- Deactivate employees who are no longer active
- Reactivate employees
- View the full employee list, including inactive employees

### Usage Context

- Uses the application on a single workstation
- Runs one instance at a time
